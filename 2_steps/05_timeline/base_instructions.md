# Step 05: Timeline (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Establish an unambiguous chronological record of character locations, actions, and object states across five scenes to ensure continuity, and that causal relationships make sense.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 bounded event window:** Track the chronological events from the time when characters enter the location until the crime occurs. Include the crime. Do not include any events after the crime, including the investigation. This is different from what a reader would observe, and it's important to consider how the truth laid out here will naturally allow for alibis and other possible interpretations, during the investigation.
- **Length cap:** 200–300 words total (do not rely on stopwatch-style minute/second math; use sequential event phases and observable transitions).
