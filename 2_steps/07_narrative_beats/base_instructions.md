# Step 07: Narrative Beats (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Expand a single scene within the scene list into a complete sequence of concrete narrative beats so a prose-drafting step can execute the scene faithfully without inventing new plot facts.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 single scene** expanded into its ordered narrative beats (approximately 8-10 beats).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md`
- `2_steps/07_narrative_beats/context.md`

## Base Deliverable Shape
1. **Scene Setup Snapshot:** Which characters are here, active physical props & changes in the location, and what the POV character believes entering the scene.
2. **Summary:** Overall purpose of the scene, its main events, and its effect on the story. Also include what has shifted in the story between the first beat and the last beat.
3. **Story Beats:** Back and forth action-reaction pairs between characters who try to advance their agendas. Each request is granted, blocked, or redirected. Each beat should include the characters involved, why it advances the story or characters, what is being requested, and the specifics of the response. Beats can be repeated (i.e. asking again and again for the same thing, and being denied every time), but they must overall smoothly guide the reader from one idea to the next.
Include the following:
  - character intent
  - interactive friction (questions, deflections, requests)
  - emotional subtext (if any)
  - effect on mystery (if any)
Each beat must have at least one of emotional subtext OR effect on mystery, but it is encouraged for beats to have both where it's impactful.
4. **Next Hook:** How the reader is guided towards the next chapter.
