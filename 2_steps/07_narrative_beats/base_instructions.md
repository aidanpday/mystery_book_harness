# Step 07: Narrative Beats (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Expand a single scene outline into a complete sequence of concrete dramatic beats so a prose-drafting step can execute the scene faithfully without inventing new plot facts.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 single scene** expanded into its ordered narrative beats (approximately 5–8 beats).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md`
- `2_steps/07_narrative_beats/context.md`

## Base Deliverable Shape
1. **Scene Setup Snapshot:** Exact starting positions, active physical props, and what `C-02` believes entering the scene.
2. **Ordered Narrative Beats:** Action-and-dialogue turns specifying character intent, interactive friction (questions, deflections, requests), exact sensory clue visibility, and emotional subtext.
3. **Ending State:** What has shifted in `C-02`'s suspicion or relationship with `C-01` by the final beat.
