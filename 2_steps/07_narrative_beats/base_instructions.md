# Step 07: Narrative Beats (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Expand a single scene within the scene list into a complete sequence of concrete narrative beats so a prose-drafting step can execute the scene faithfully without inventing new plot facts.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 single scene** expanded into its ordered narrative beats (approximately 8–10 beats).
- **Word-count allocation:** Spend **100–150 words** on the overall Scene Setup & Summary (the big-picture view of the scene's purpose, starting state, main events, and what shifts by the end), and **up to 150–200 words total** across the ordered Story Beats and Next Hook.
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total (bulk of the word count for the big-picture view, concise entries for the individual beats).

## Grounded Calibration (5/10 Target)
- Produce grounded, natural interpersonal beats without imposing heavy melodrama or over-complicated machinations.
- Use simple, plain language to describe functional actions, clear questions, and everyday social friction that are easy to imagine.
- On a 1–10 scale for **melodrama**, **complexity**, and **non-functional detail** (where outputs often drift to 7/10 melodramatic, 7/10 complicated, and 6/10 non-functional), aim squarely for **5/10 in all three categories**.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md`
- `2_steps/07_narrative_beats/context.md`

## Base Deliverable Shape
1. **Scene Overview & Summary (100–150 words):** Overall purpose of the scene, active characters and props, what the POV character believes entering the scene, main events, and what has shifted between the first beat and the last beat.
2. **Story Beats & Hook (150–200 words total):**
   - **Story Beats:** Concise back-and-forth action-reaction pairs between characters trying to advance their agendas (request granted, blocked, or redirected). Include character intent, interactive friction (questions, deflections, requests), and at least one of emotional subtext OR effect on mystery.
   - **Next Hook:** How the reader is guided naturally toward the next chapter.
