# Step 08: Scene Draft (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Draft reader-facing narrative prose that faithfully renders a fixed sequence of narrative beats without introducing unapproved plot elements or internal planning labels.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 concise drafted scene slice** executing the fixed beat sheet in `context.md`.
- **Length cap:** 350–500 words of final reader-facing prose.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md` (Full prose rules, situational rules, tonal target 40%, and blacklist)
- `2_steps/08_scene_draft/context.md`

## Base Deliverable Shape
- Pure reader-facing narrative prose in close third-person POV (`C-02`'s character name from `context.md`).
- Zero internal labels (`C-01`, `Zone B`, `MacGuffin`), zero headers or beat numbers, and strictly zero new characters, unapproved jargon, or invented plot twists beyond `context.md`.
