# Step 01: Setting (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Define the bounded "bottle" setting for the mystery and detail a single representative zone within it.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the bounded location (entry/exit constraints, journey or containment window, and why the two brief darkness disruptions occur naturally within this environment).
- **1 detailed zone** within that location (physical layout, normal social use, sensory atmosphere, and how movement or visibility works there).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md` (Target tone & universal bans)

## Base Deliverable Shape
1. **Bounded Location Concept:** Premise, entry/exit boundary, and natural cause of the two disruptions.
2. **Sample Zone Profile:** Simple name/role of the zone, normal occupant behavior, tactile/sensory traits, and physical constraints relevant to fair-play observation.
