# Step 01: Setting (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Define the bounded "bottle" setting for the mystery and detail a single representative zone within it.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 Bounded Location Concept (100–150 words):** Overall concept and summary of the location, who is present, and the natural or social parameters for entering and leaving.
- **1 Sample Zone Profile (3 subsections, up to 100 words each):** Physical layout, normal social use for different types of people inside, and where clues could be observed.
- **Length cap:** 250–400 words total (spend the bulk of the word count on the big-picture concept and summary, and a smaller portion on each of the 3 detail subsections).

## Grounded Calibration (5/10 Target)
- Produce grounded locations without trying to impose heavy drama or themes onto the space.
- Use simple, plain language to describe functional spaces that are easy to imagine.
- On a 1–10 scale for **melodrama**, **complexity**, and **non-functional detail** (where early outputs drifted to 7/10 melodramatic, 7/10 complicated, and 6/10 non-functional), aim squarely for **5/10 in all three categories**.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md` (Target tone & universal bans)

## Base Deliverable Shape
1. **Bounded Location Concept (100–150 words):** Summary of the overall setting concept, normal activity, and realistic entry/exit or social containment boundary.
2. **Sample Zone Profile (up to 100 words per subsection):**
   - **Role & Layout:** Simple name, everyday function, and clear physical layout of the zone.
   - **Occupant Dynamics:** Normal behavior and movement of the different roles who use this space.
   - **Observation & Clues:** Everyday sightlines, acoustics, and routine objects relevant to fair-play observation.
