# Step 06: Scene List (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Translate chronological list of events into a reader-facing sequence of scenes (syuzhet) that controls pacing, chapter-ending hooks, intrigue, and flow.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **A sequence of 2–3 consecutive scenes** spanning the characters entering the location until the crime is discovered. This includes when the crime is committed, but the details of it should remain outside the scope of the scenes.
- **Word-count allocation:** Spend **100–150 words** on the overall sequence overview and summary (how the 2–3 scenes build momentum from normal arrival to discovery), and **up to 70–80 words** on each individual scene breakdown below.
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total (bulk of the word count for the big-picture view, smaller portion for the per-scene details).

## Grounded Calibration (5/10 Target)
- Produce grounded, functional scene progressions without imposing heavy melodrama, artificial thematic portent, or convoluted staging.
- Use simple, plain language to describe clear scene actions and social situations that are easy to imagine.
- On a 1–10 scale for **melodrama**, **complexity**, and **non-functional detail** (where outputs often drift to 7/10 melodramatic, 7/10 complicated, and 6/10 non-functional), aim squarely for **5/10 in all three categories**.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md`
- `2_steps/06_scene_list/context.md`

## Base Deliverable Shape
1. **Sequence Overview & Summary (100–150 words):** Big-picture summary of the 2–3 scene arc, how normal social dynamics are established, and how the transition to the crime discovery unfolds.
2. **Per-Scene Breakdown (up to 70–80 words per scene for 2–3 scenes):**
   - **Scene Events:** Location zone, characters present, and any clues or knowledge the reader learns.
   - **Dramatic Purpose:** How this scene advances character dynamics and grounded narrative tension.
   - **Exit Hook:** A natural turning point that propels the reader into the next scene.
