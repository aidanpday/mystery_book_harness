# Step 04: Clues & Misdirection — Syuzhet (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Design how a single deduction in the investigation is planted fairly for the reader (syuzhet) while misdirecting initial suspicion. This is a description of the order in which a reader would learn information about a crime, from investigation to clues to interpretation to reality.

State events in order in a matter-of-fact, simple outline.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 complete clue chain:** At least 2 clues (max 4) that have several possible interpretations but, through deduction and investigation, result in some solution. It does not have to solve the entire mystery, only part of it.
- **Length cap:** 200–300 words total.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md`
- `2_steps/04_clues_syuzhet/context.md`

## Base Deliverable Shape
1. **Target Deduction:** The single factual conclusion this clue chain proves.
2. **Clues:** Exact details the investigator would find and their possible interpretations.
3. **Misdirection:** Why an attentive reader may consider at first some conclusions that aren't the right one.
4. **Reframing Trigger:** What later context uncovers the clear meaning of the clue, in retrospect.
