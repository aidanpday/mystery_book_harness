# Step 04: Clues & Misdirection — Syuzhet (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Design how a single deduction in the investigation is planted fairly for the reader (syuzhet) while misdirecting initial suspicion.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 complete clue chain:** 1–2 observable sensory or conversational clues planted before the reveal, the false interpretation that initially misdirects `C-02` (and the reader), and the true deduction unlocked later.
- **Length cap:** 200–300 words total.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md`
- `2_steps/04_clues_syuzhet/context.md`

## Base Deliverable Shape
1. **Target Deduction:** The single factual conclusion this clue chain proves.
2. **Observable Plant(s):** Exact sensory detail or conversational contradiction witnessed by `C-02` (no off-screen knowledge or technical measurements).
3. **Misdirection:** Why an attentive reader and `C-02` naturally draw the wrong conclusion at first.
4. **Reframing Trigger:** What later context flips the meaning of the planted clue so the truth clicks in retrospect.
