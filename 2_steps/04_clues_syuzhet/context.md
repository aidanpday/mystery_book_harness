# Fixed Upstream Context (Step 04 Fixture)

Use these fixed baseline assumptions so all 10 prototypes in an iteration test clue design against identical story facts (replace with converged outputs from Steps 01–03 when ready):

- **Bounded Setting:** An enclosed journey/gathering with two predictable brief blackouts (First Disruption and Second Disruption) and three adjacent zones: a public common area (`Zone A`), a restricted storage/prep area (`Zone B`), and private quarters/corridor (`Zone C`).
- **Cast Roles:**
  - `C-01`: Charming, respected insider smuggling a counterfeit MacGuffin; recruits `C-02` to investigate its theft.
  - `C-02`: Observant lower-status protagonist narrating from close third-person perspective.
  - `C-03`: Accomplished peer of `C-01` who steals the MacGuffin during the First Disruption to prove it is counterfeit before arrival.
  - `C-04`: Uninvolved bystander hiding an unrelated personal indiscretion in `Zone B` during the First Disruption.
- **Target Crime Fact to Clue:** During the First Disruption, `C-03` took the MacGuffin from `Zone B`, while `C-01` deliberately delayed raising the alarm so no one would inspect the empty container too closely.
