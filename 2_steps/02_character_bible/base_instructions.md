# Step 02: Character Bible (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Create a character bible entry that defines who a character is, why they naturally belong in the bounded location, and how they interact with others under pressure.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 single character** who might be present inside a location as a guest, worker, or travelling expert.
- **Length cap:** 200–300 words total.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md` (Target tone & universal bans—strictly zero financial motives or legal technicalities)

## Base Deliverable Shape
1. **Role & Plain Label:** Simple, jargon-free everyday role and social standing.
2. **Reason in Location:** Normal, believable reason for entering the bounded setting.
3. **Competence & Blind Spot:** Practical domain of competence (governing what actions are plausible for them) and their personal flaws or blind spots.
4. **Motive / Secret:** Stake driving their choices during the disruptions.
5. **Voice & Interpersonal Cadence:** How they speak, ask questions, or deflect in conversation.
