# Step 02: Character Bible (Base Instructions)

## Goal
Create a character bible entry that defines who a character is, why they naturally belong in the bounded location, and how they interact with others under pressure.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 single character** (default target: `C-01`, `C-02`, `C-03`, or one additional bystander suspect `C-04`, held constant across all 10 prototypes in an iteration; default to `C-01` unless specified otherwise).
- **Length cap:** 200–300 words total.

## Fixed Inputs
- `1_harness/01_project.md`
- `1_harness/02_goals.md`
- `1_harness/03_style.md` (Target tone & universal bans—strictly zero financial motives or legal technicalities)

## Base Deliverable Shape
1. **Role & Plain Label:** Simple, jargon-free everyday role and social standing relative to `C-02`.
2. **Reason in Location:** Normal, believable reason for entering and remaining in the bounded setting.
3. **Competence & Blind Spot:** Practical domain of competence (governing what actions are plausible for them) and key personal flaw or blind spot.
4. **Motive / Secret:** Non-financial stake driving their choices during the disruptions.
5. **Voice & Interpersonal Cadence:** How they speak, ask questions, or deflect in conversation.
