# Step 01: Setting — Direction 01 (Island Expedition Radio Tower)

## Goal
Define a bounded "bottle" setting and detail a single representative zone for an isolated island research expedition whose only link to the outside world is a high-frequency radio mast.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** An isolated island scientific field station where researchers report findings back to the mainland across strictly scheduled radio transmission windows.
- **Craft Emulation:** Dashiell Hammett (*The Maltese Falcon*) paired with Maurice Leblanc (*Arsène Lupin*)—brisk, articulate dialogue, precise technical protocols, and tight spatial misdirection around transmission schedules.
- **Containment Mechanism:** Coastal isolation cut off by rough tides and gale conditions; the radio tower is the station's sole real-time contact with headquarters.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the bounded station (geographic isolation, scheduled transmission cadence, why no one can depart).
- **1 detailed zone** within the compound (e.g., the Transmission Desk & Equipment Room: generator hum, transmitter dials, physical log sheets, line-of-sight sightlines).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Universal Scientific Controls
- **Tone:** 40% on the stylistic calibration scale (crisp, witty, articulate whodunnit; no melodrama; no farce).
- **Universal Bans:** Strictly zero mentions of money, budgets, grants, or financial gain; zero legal technicalities or bureaucratic loopholes.
- **Fair-Play Clues:** Ground spatial clues in human sight and sound (switch toggles, logbook pencil notations, cable paths, window reflections). Avoid micro-measurements and tactile/temperature sensory indulgence.
- **Style Blacklist:** Strictly ban all blacklisted words from `1_harness/03_style.md` (e.g., *humming, hum, palpable, tapestry, testament, unyielding, structural, shiver down the spine, cacophony, unspoken, visceral, labyrinthine, dance of shadows, pregnant pause, chill in the air, needle in a haystack, echoing silence, mechanics, mechanical, automatic, automatically, operational*).
