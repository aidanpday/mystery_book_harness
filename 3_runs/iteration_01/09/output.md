### Bounded Location Concept: Mount Platå Seed Vault
Carved into the sandstone bedrock of a Svalbard mountain, the Mount Platå Seed Vault safeguards regional crop varieties inside sub-zero permafrost caverns. A fierce equinoctial blizzard has battered the mountain face, freezing the dual-hydraulic exterior portal doors behind an impenetrable sheet of rime. Until the relief icebreaker arrives at sunrise twelve hours away, exiting into the gale is lethal. Confined inside are five specialists—visiting agronomists, the facility registrar, and a lone maintenance engineer—whose academic rivalries now unfold in close isolation.

### Sample Zone Profile: Acclimatization Airlock & Sorting Atrium
- **Role & Layout:** Situated between the heated living quarters and the frozen seed caverns, this concrete staging hall acts as the primary thermal barrier. A row of numbered steel lockers holds down parkas, fleece hoods, and rubber boots. Heavy dual-latch gasketed doors stand at opposite ends, while a double-paned observation window overlooks a stainless steel sorting bench illuminated by clear fluorescent tubes.
- **Occupant Dynamics:** Passing between the living sector and the frozen vaults requires an exacting dressing routine. Researchers exchange sharp critiques of each other's field notes while pulling on insulated gear and checking locker tags. Vault regulations dictate entering through the gasketed doors individually to prevent warm air currents from disturbing the frost. Routine sorting tasks keep the scientists traversing this space in predictable shifts.
- **Observation & Clue Mechanics:** The sharp thermal gradient provides visible, auditory clues for an observant investigator. Condensation rapidly fogs the inspection window whenever someone exits the warm quarters. Thawing boot prints leave dark outlines across the heated floor, exposing distinctive tread patterns and routes. Frost crystallizes on cold locker handles touched by bare fingers, while mismatched parka tags on wall hooks and the metallic click of the vault latch reveal who accessed the frozen repository.
