# Step 01: Setting — Direction 09 (Permafrost Arctic Seed Repository)

## Goal
Define a bounded "bottle" setting and detail a single representative zone inside a mountain permafrost seed repository sealed against a polar storm.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** A global seed archive carved into sandstone bedrock within the Arctic permafrost zone. Visiting agronomists, catalogers, and maintenance engineers are confined within the facility while a polar blizzard freezes the exterior portal airlocks shut until sunrise.
- **Craft Emulation:** Peter Høeg (*Smilla's Sense of Snow*)—grounding every observation in environmental cold literacy, condensation behavior, thermal outerwear habits, and the physical interaction between human breath and frozen surfaces.
- **Containment Mechanism:** The heavy dual-hydraulic mountain portal doors are sealed shut against hurricane-force Arctic winds; stepping outside into sub-zero whiteout conditions without transport is lethal.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the underground seed repository (bedrock permafrost vaults, exterior airlock seal, storm duration).
- **1 detailed zone** between living and frozen sectors (e.g., The Acclimatization Airlock & Sorting Atrium: concrete staging hall, numbered parka lockers, frosted viewing panes, airlock latches).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Universal Scientific Controls
- **Tone:** 40% on the stylistic calibration scale (crisp, witty, intelligent whodunnit; articulate scientists; no grim survival melodrama).
- **Universal Bans:** Strictly zero mentions of research grants, commercial patent rights, asset valuations, or money; zero international treaty statutes or bureaucratic loopholes.
- **Fair-Play Clues:** Ground clues in physical sight and sound—frost accumulation on locker hinges, numbered parka swaps, breath vapor visibility, boot track thaw marks on warm concrete, and double-latch clicks.
- **Style Blacklist:** Strictly ban all blacklisted words from `1_harness/03_style.md` (e.g., *humming, hum, palpable, tapestry, testament, unyielding, structural, shiver down the spine, cacophony, unspoken, visceral, labyrinthine, dance of shadows, pregnant pause, chill in the air, needle in a haystack, echoing silence, mechanics, mechanical, automatic, automatically, operational*).

Deliver strictly into `output.md` with no meta-commentary, preambles, or markdown placeholders:
