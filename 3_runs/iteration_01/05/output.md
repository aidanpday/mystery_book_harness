### Bounded Location Concept: Maison Vaneau
Maison Vaneau occupies an enclosed Parisian townhouse shuttered against the street by iron courtyard gates and bolted oak blinds. To protect the autumn collection from rival houses before dawn, master couturier Hector Vaneau enforces an absolute overnight sequester. The solitary courtyard gate key remains locked inside his mahogany desk until the morning press courier arrives at seven o'clock. Six colleagues remain sealed inside to complete the gowns: two cutters, three fitters, and a mannequin model. They work through the night under relentless deadline pressure, bound together by artistic vanity and mutual creative distrust.

### Sample Zone Profile: The Mirror Fitting Salon & Pinning Loft
- **Role & Layout:** The ground salon features high ceilings, polished parquet, and three velvet dressing screens beside an angled triptych mirror. A wrought-iron spiral staircase ascends to an open pinning loft directly overhead. From the loft railing, an observer looks down into the mirror glass to inspect the curtained fitting alcoves below.
- **Occupant Dynamics:** Seamstresses hurry between the pressing tables and the velvet dais with basting needles held between their lips. House models stand upon the platform trading crisp barbs over posture and drape. Senior cutters circle the floor with heavy shears, adjusting silhouettes and defending their pattern choices with acid wit.
- **Observation & Clue Mechanics:** Dropped brass pins across the oak floor indicate where a person paused in the dark. French chalk rubbed off on skirting boards reveals silent passage behind the screens. Angled mirror sightlines allow someone in the loft to witness unseen movements, while indigo fabric dye on a linen cuff exposes who handled the secret bridal gown.
