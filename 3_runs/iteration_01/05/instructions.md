# Step 01: Setting — Direction 05 (Haute-Couture Fashion Atelier)

## Goal
Define a bounded "bottle" setting and detail a single representative zone inside an insular Parisian-style haute-couture fashion salon locked down overnight before an exclusive dawn collection preview.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** An eccentric, high-pressure creative atelier where lead designers, seamstresses, pattern-cutters, and house models are sequestered to finish gowns and protect secret collection designs from leaks before the morning press showing.
- **Craft Emulation:** Margery Allingham (*Flowers for the Judge*)—eccentric creative pride, sharp verbal repartee, intense workspace intimacy, and keen observational deduction among colleagues with long-standing rivalries.
- **Containment Mechanism:** Heavy iron exterior courtyard shutters bolted and sealed from within; the street door key remains locked in the head couturier’s desk until the morning courier arrives.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the locked atelier (the pre-dawn deadline, design secrecy, physical containment behind courtyard shutters).
- **1 detailed zone** within the salon (e.g., The Mirror Fitting Salon & Pinning Loft: triptych mirrors, elevated spiral stair, velvet draping, pinning mannequins).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Universal Scientific Controls
- **Tone:** 40% on the stylistic calibration scale (crisp, witty, stylish whodunnit; articulate artistic bickering; no campy caricature or melodrama).
- **Universal Bans:** Strictly zero mentions of dress prices, commercial sales, bankruptcy, or financial stakes; zero copyright laws or corporate legal threats.
- **Fair-Play Clues:** Ground clues in dropped tailoring chalk, silk pin boxes, thread shears, fabric dye marks on cuffs, and angled mirror reflections across corners.
- **Style Blacklist:** Strictly ban all blacklisted words from `1_harness/03_style.md` (e.g., *humming, hum, palpable, tapestry, testament, unyielding, structural, shiver down the spine, cacophony, unspoken, visceral, labyrinthine, dance of shadows, pregnant pause, chill in the air, needle in a haystack, echoing silence, mechanics, mechanical, automatic, automatically, operational*).
