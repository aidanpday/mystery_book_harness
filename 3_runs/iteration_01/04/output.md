### Bounded Location Concept: Restaurant Moreau
The kitchen of Restaurant Moreau is bound not by iron bolts, but by an uninterrupted three-hour dinner rush. From seven until ten in the evening, ninety covers demand synchronized perfection across five line stations. Abandoning a sauté pan or walking out the service entrance brings immediate ruin to a course and instant public disgrace in the dining room. Escoffier's brigade hierarchy enforces strict subordination. The executive chef commands from the center. Line cooks and kitchen runners remain pinned to their posts by sizzling pans and the relentless stream of incoming orders.

### Sample Zone Profile: The Expediter's Pass and Plating Island
- **Role & Layout:** Positioned at the exact fulcrum of the kitchen, this stainless-steel counter divides the cooking line from the waitstaff corridor. Two overhead infrared heat lamps bathe the marble finishing slab in amber light. A brass ticket spindle sits beside a sixty-slot carousel rail. From this elevated post, the expediter maintains an unobstructed line of sight across the range toward the cold larder and the dining room doors.
- **Occupant Dynamics:** The sous chef calls out ticket numbers in crisp cadences. Sauté cooks pivot on rubber floor mats to slide searing copper pans onto the pass. Plating requires swift cooperation, as line cooks garnish roasted squab with silver tweezers before the expediter inspects every porcelain rim. Service runners collect completed silver trays within fifteen seconds of the order bell, keeping the kitchen floor in constant, ordered circulation.
- **Observation & Clue Mechanics:** The paper ticket track creates an unalterable chronological record of every table. Two dual-dial kitchen timers chime at precise five-minute intervals, providing auditory markers for the entire line. Rogue salt spills on the black marble slab mark hasty plating. A missing pair of offset plating tweezers or an unwashed copper reduction spoon left beside the pastry bain-marie offers clear evidence of unauthorized station visits.
