# Step 01: Setting — Direction 04 (Commercial Kitchen Dinner Rush)

## Goal
Define a bounded "bottle" setting and detail a single representative zone inside a prestigious restaurant kitchen held captive by a relentless, high-stakes dinner rush.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** The kitchen brigade is not physically locked behind bolted doors; instead, they are held hostage by the operational pressure of a packed dining room during an uninterrupted dinner service rush. Leaving a station or abandoning a course causes immediate culinary collapse and public exposure.
- **Craft Emulation:** Rex Stout (*Too Many Cooks*)—gastronomic precision, sensory timing, strict kitchen hierarchy, and the coordinated dance of line stations as an alibi framework.
- **Containment Mechanism:** The relentless flow of ticket orders, timed oven pans, and the presence of service runners ferrying plates through the swinging double doors to the dining room floor.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the commercial kitchen during the peak service window (the operational containment, the brigade hierarchy, the duration of dinner service).
- **1 detailed zone** at the center of the line (e.g., The Pass & Line Prep Island: ticket rails, heat lamps, stainless steel prep counters, line of sight to cold larder and range).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Universal Scientific Controls
- **Tone:** 40% on the stylistic calibration scale (crisp, witty, brisk whodunnit; sharp professional banter; no chaotic slapstick).
- **Universal Bans:** Strictly zero mentions of restaurant profit, tips, payroll, food costs, or financial leverage; zero health-code statutes or regulatory violations.
- **Fair-Play Clues:** Ground clues in physical sight and sound—order ticket sequences, timer bells, pan spatters, plating tweezers, spice dusting on marble slabs, and swinging door visibility.
- **Style Blacklist:** Strictly ban all blacklisted words from `1_harness/03_style.md` (e.g., *humming, hum, palpable, tapestry, testament, unyielding, structural, shiver down the spine, cacophony, unspoken, visceral, labyrinthine, dance of shadows, pregnant pause, chill in the air, needle in a haystack, echoing silence, mechanics, mechanical, automatic, automatically, operational*).
