# Step 01: Setting — Direction 06 (Gothic Hall & Medieval Apothecary)

## Goal
Define a bounded "bottle" setting and detail a single representative zone inside a fortified medieval stone apothecary and monastic gothic hall.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** A heavy stone-walled cloistered apothecary and gothic hall. Rather than leaning on delicate herbal aromas or sensory botanicals, the setting emphasizes monumental architectural mass—groined stone vaults, heavy timber drying presses, stone mortars, and locked wrought-iron compound cabinets.
- **Craft Emulation:** Ellis Peters (*A Morbid Taste for Bones*)—scholarly discipline, cloistered routine, articulate academic rivalries, and clear spatial boundaries within an ancient foundation.
- **Containment Mechanism:** The heavy oak-and-iron cloister gates are locked for the night during an equinox conservation symposium, with stone walls and high slit windows preventing departure until morning matins.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the walled compound (the monastic stone perimeter, nocturnal gate curfew, scholarly gathering).
- **1 detailed zone** within the gothic structure (e.g., The Vaulted Still-Room & Herbarium Hall: carved stone corbels, massive timber presses, flagstone floors, central mortar bench).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Universal Scientific Controls
- **Tone:** 40% on the stylistic calibration scale (crisp, witty, intelligent whodunnit; articulate scholarly debate; no gothic horror or supernatural melodrama).
- **Universal Bans:** Strictly zero mentions of money, bequest values, commerce, or financial gain; zero monastic canon law technicalities or legal statutes.
- **Fair-Play Clues:** Ground clues in stone architecture, physical sight and sound—flagstone footstep reverberations, chalked bin marks, ground mineral residue on mortar pestles, iron latch positions, and candle lantern angles. Avoid subjective scent immersion.
- **Style Blacklist:** Strictly ban all blacklisted words from `1_harness/03_style.md` (e.g., *humming, hum, palpable, tapestry, testament, unyielding, structural, shiver down the spine, cacophony, unspoken, visceral, labyrinthine, dance of shadows, pregnant pause, chill in the air, needle in a haystack, echoing silence, mechanics, mechanical, automatic, automatically, operational*).
