# Step 01: Setting — Direction 07 (Decommissioned Bunker Readiness Drill)

## Goal
Define a bounded "bottle" setting and detail a single representative zone inside a decommissioned Cold-War subterranean bunker during an overnight readiness drill featuring historical reenactors and academic archaeologists.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** A sealed civil heritage bunker where two clashing factions spend the night: historical reenactors obsessed with rigid military drill protocol, and academic archaeologists documenting physical hardware and vintage communication systems.
- **Craft Emulation:** Dorothy L. Sayers (*The Nine Tailors*)—integrating the physical systems, operational protocols, and architectural quirks of a monumental specialized structure directly into the mystery puzzle.
- **Containment Mechanism:** A timed thirty-ton steel blast door sealed for a twelve-hour heritage preservation test; the mechanical clockwork lock cannot release until eight o'clock in the morning.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the underground bunker (the timed lockdown, ventilation schedules, the social friction between reenactors in uniform and field archaeologists).
- **1 detailed zone** within the facility (e.g., The Central Plotting Map Room & Gallery: sunken command pit, Perspex map tables, upper observation glass balcony, vintage speaking tubes).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Universal Scientific Controls
- **Tone:** 40% on the stylistic calibration scale (crisp, witty, articulate whodunnit; sharp friction between procedural reenactors and scholarly curators; no paranoid thriller tropes).
- **Universal Bans:** Strictly zero mentions of budget allocations, salaries, asset liquidation, or financial motives; zero military penal codes or bureaucratic statute loopholes.
- **Fair-Play Clues:** Ground clues in magnetic map markers, speaking-tube acoustic leaks, grease pencils on clear Perspex, rubber mat impressions, and switchboard plug configurations.
- **Style Blacklist:** Strictly ban all blacklisted words from `1_harness/03_style.md` (e.g., *humming, hum, palpable, tapestry, testament, unyielding, structural, shiver down the spine, cacophony, unspoken, visceral, labyrinthine, dance of shadows, pregnant pause, chill in the air, needle in a haystack, echoing silence, mechanics, mechanical, automatic, automatically, operational*).
