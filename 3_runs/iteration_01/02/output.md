### Bounded Location Concept: The Minerva Theatre (*The Glass Parlour*)
The evening performance of *The Glass Parlour* at the Minerva Theatre has reached Act III without an intermission. Five hundred theatergoers sit in the auditorium, blocking the front exits, while the house manager latches the alley stage door to seal out street noise until the final curtain call. An authentic murder has occurred in the second scene. The lead actor, portraying Inspector Sterling, must deduce the killer among his castmates without missing a cue or breaking character. Walking offstage ruins the show, forcing the ensemble to disguise genuine panic and whispered cross-examinations as improvised dramatic tension.

### Sample Zone Profile: Stage Left Wing and Prompt Corner
- **Role & Layout:** Tucked behind the canvas flat of the drawing-room fireplace, the prompt corner commands a direct sightline into the illuminated stage center. A slanted mahogany prompt desk holds the cue script, red and green warning lamps, and the headset station. Two paces upstage, a felt-lined prop table sits beneath shaded blue work lights, framed by velvet drapes that funnel traffic toward the stage door vestibule.
- **Occupant Dynamics:** Performers hover in the narrow corridor between scenes, observing the prompt book's rigid cue cadence. Dressers manage fifteen-second quick-changes under strict hush rules, slipping actors into coats while dialogue carries past the footlights. With a real crime discovered, cast members trade tense inquiries during costume shifts, whispering accusations between rehearsed exits while maintaining stage poise before stepping back onto the boards.
- **Observation & Clue Mechanics:** Fair deductions hinge on visible theatrical details. A misplaced brooch on the green felt prop table reveals an unscripted backstage detour. Fresh yellow chalk spikes mark an altered blocking position near the prompt drape, exposing where someone stood out of sight. A green cue lamp flickers out of sequence during a monologue, while an actor's live microphone transmits an illicit whisper to the prompt corner monitor speakers.
