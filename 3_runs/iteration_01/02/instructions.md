# Step 01: Setting — Direction 02 (Live Repertory Play Mid-Performance)

## Goal
Define a bounded "bottle" setting and detail a single representative zone for a live theatrical play where an active performance traps the cast onstage and backstage before an audience.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** A regular stage play running before a live audience. During the show, the actor playing the investigator discovers a real crime has occurred backstage or onstage. The cast must adapt, improvise around missed cues, and identify the culprit without breaking character, while the audience treats every deviation and whispered panic as part of the brilliant staging.
- **Craft Emulation:** Ngaio Marsh (*Opening Night*)—theatrical discipline, stage hierarchies, cue sheets, and backstage traffic routines that mask deliberate sabotage inside rehearsed choreography.
- **Containment Mechanism:** The unrelenting runtime of the live performance. Walking offstage ruins the show, the exterior stage door is guarded by the house manager, and the audience blocks the front exit until the final curtain call.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the theater setting (the active performance constraint, front-of-house vs. backstage boundary, the imperative to finish the third act).
- **1 detailed zone** at the boundary of performance and reality (e.g., The Stage Wings & Prompt Corner: sightlines through scenic flats, prompt desk, prop tables, monitor speakers).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Universal Scientific Controls
- **Tone:** 40% on the stylistic calibration scale (crisp, witty, articulate whodunnit; sharp comic irony between onstage drama and real investigation; no slapstick farce).
- **Universal Bans:** Strictly zero mentions of ticket sales, box office receipts, wages, or financial motives; zero legal gotchas or police jurisdiction debates.
- **Fair-Play Clues:** Ground clues in visible cue lights, prop table discrepancies, whispered dialogue audible over body mics, chalk floor spikes, and quick-change timings.
- **Style Blacklist:** Strictly ban all blacklisted words from `1_harness/03_style.md` (e.g., *humming, hum, palpable, tapestry, testament, unyielding, structural, shiver down the spine, cacophony, unspoken, visceral, labyrinthine, dance of shadows, pregnant pause, chill in the air, needle in a haystack, echoing silence, mechanics, mechanical, automatic, automatically, operational*).
