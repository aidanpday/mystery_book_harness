# Step 01: Setting — Direction 08 (Offshore Commercial Fishing Vessel)

## Goal
Define a bounded "bottle" setting and detail a single representative zone aboard a commercial deep-sea fishing vessel on a continuous two-day voyage out to sea.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** An offshore commercial fishing boat operating forty miles off the coast on a grueling forty-eight-hour voyage. The crew and onboard observers are locked into round-the-clock watch rotations, net hauls, and sorting shifts.
- **Craft Emulation:** Georges Simenon (*The Lock at Charenton*)—patient observation of working-class tradecraft, damp maritime atmosphere, tacit habits, and quiet behavioural contradictions under physical strain.
- **Containment Mechanism:** Absolute geographic containment—surrounded on all sides by open deep-sea ocean with no tenders, lifeboats reserved for survival emergencies, and two full days until docking.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the trawler (ship dimensions, two-day sea schedule, isolation on the fishing banks).
- **1 detailed zone** aboard ship (e.g., The Wheelhouse & Trawl Sorting Deck: raised navigation bridge overlooking the illuminated working deck, hydraulic winch controls, wet sorting troughs).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Universal Scientific Controls
- **Tone:** 40% on the stylistic calibration scale (crisp, witty, grounded whodunnit; laconic, capable deckhands and officers; no overwrought sea-horror).
- **Universal Bans:** Strictly zero mentions of catch values, market prices, crew shares, or money; zero maritime law codes or regulatory inspections.
- **Fair-Play Clues:** Ground clues in visible winch cable tensions, sea-boot prints on salted decks, wheelhouse brass compass reflections, wet oilskin locker arrangements, and deck light sightlines.
- **Style Blacklist:** Strictly ban all blacklisted words from `1_harness/03_style.md` (e.g., *humming, hum, palpable, tapestry, testament, unyielding, structural, shiver down the spine, cacophony, unspoken, visceral, labyrinthine, dance of shadows, pregnant pause, chill in the air, needle in a haystack, echoing silence, mechanics, mechanical, automatic, automatically, operational*).
