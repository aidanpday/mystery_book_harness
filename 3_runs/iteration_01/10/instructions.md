# Step 01: Setting — Direction 10 (Hydroelectric Gorge Dam Infrastructure)

## Goal
Define a bounded "bottle" setting and detail a single representative zone inside a canyon hydroelectric dam, emphasizing monumental concrete infrastructure over administrative paperwork.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** A massive curved concrete dam wall wedged between sheer canyon cliffs. Focus heavily on the monumental physical infrastructure—crest roadway, hollow inspection galleries carved through the concrete monolith, penstock shafts, and vast turbine halls—rather than shift logbooks.
- **Craft Emulation:** Georges Simenon paired with John Buchan (*The Thirty-Nine Steps*)—contrasting grand physical scale with crisp, observant deduction across echoing catwalks and vertical corridors.
- **Containment Mechanism:** Seasonal spillway discharge gates have opened to relieve reservoir floodwaters, drowning the single canyon access road downstream and isolating the dam until water levels draw down at daybreak.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the dam complex (canyon geography, flooding containment window, monumental concrete mass).
- **1 detailed zone** connecting vertical levels (e.g., The Terrazzo Turbine Overlook & Penstock Inspection Gallery: high brass-railed balcony looking down on generator casings, heavy intake valves, access spiral to internal gallery).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Universal Scientific Controls
- **Tone:** 40% on the stylistic calibration scale (crisp, witty, puzzle whodunnit; articulate engineers and visitors; no action-thriller disaster tropes).
- **Universal Bans:** Strictly zero mentions of utility budgets, electricity revenue, municipal contracts, or money; zero regulatory citations or legal liability clauses.
- **Fair-Play Clues:** Ground clues in visible infrastructure geometry—brass handrail reflections, catwalk shadows, grease marks on turbine access hatches, flashlight sweeps through damp inspection galleries, and valve wheel positions.
- **Style Blacklist:** Strictly ban all blacklisted words from `1_harness/03_style.md` (e.g., *humming, hum, palpable, tapestry, testament, unyielding, structural, shiver down the spine, cacophony, unspoken, visceral, labyrinthine, dance of shadows, pregnant pause, chill in the air, needle in a haystack, echoing silence, mechanics, mechanical, automatic, automatically, operational*).
