### Bounded Location Concept: Kestrel Gorge Dam
Wedged between sheer basalt cliffs, Kestrel Gorge Dam holds a deep reservoir behind a massive curved concrete wall. The canyon road downstream remains flooded each spring during scheduled spillway discharge. Heavy floodgates open at dusk to relieve snowmelt, plunging water into the gorge and isolating the crest roadway until daybreak. Only the dam crest and its concrete monolith remain dry. Staff and invited inspection guests dwell inside a closed concrete redoubt suspended between two granite walls.

### Sample Zone Profile: Terrazzo Turbine Overlook & Penstock Spiral
- **Role & Layout:** High above the turbine hall floor, a broad balcony paved in green terrazzo overlooks the row of generator casings. Polished brass handrails border the walkway, reflecting electric lanterns from the ceiling girders. At the south corner, a heavy steel portal opens onto an iron spiral staircase. The spiral descends through the concrete mass into a damp penstock gallery lined with intake valves.
- **Occupant Dynamics:** Senior engineers and civilian visitors traverse the terrazzo overlook to watch the spinning rotor shafts. Chief operators pace the floor with brass clipboards, consulting gauges before descending the spiral to verify bearing seals. Visiting guests lean over the railing for panoramic views, while junior oilers move across the generator floor below. Foot traffic funnels through the south portal, creating regular encounters between guests and technical staff.
- **Observation & Clue Mechanics:** The high balcony provides clean vertical sightlines over the generator floor, revealing anyone who approaches the turbine hatchways. Handrail brass mirrors lantern beams across the dark spiral stairwell, exposing movement below without alerting the climber. Footsteps strike metal catwalks with sharp ringing notes that pinpoint a climber's level. Graphite grease smudges on hatch handles, chalk marks on intake valve wheels, and flashlight beams cutting through gallery mist offer crisp physical trails.
