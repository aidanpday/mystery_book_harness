### Bounded Location Concept: The Arlberg Direct
The Arlberg Direct is a high-speed express train traversing steep mountain viaducts on a non-stop three-hour run. Exterior doors are locked shut with a conductor key at departure, and sealed panoramic windows bar exit against mountain drafts. The train does not halt at any intermediate stations along the alpine line. Passengers cannot board or disembark while the cars hurtle across deep river chasms. A sudden crime committed shortly after departure forces an immediate inquiry, which must finish before the express arrives at the terminus.

### Sample Zone Profile: The Observation Lounge Car
- **Role & Layout:** Positioned at the center of the passenger consist, the car features curved panoramic glass arcing into the ceiling. A central wool carpet aisle runs between fixed velvet banquettes and a polished walnut beverage bar. Heavy hinged double doors with sprung brass latches seal both ends against drafts from adjoining sleeper cars. Polished brass racks above each booth hold hats and dispatch cases in plain view.
- **Occupant Dynamics:** Articulate travellers mingle over hot tea and card games during the mountain ascent. Attentive stewards patrol the aisle on a steady five-minute circuit to clear used glassware. Because the lounge car sits between the sleeping berths and the dining saloon, every passenger walking along the train must cross this shared floor. Casual remarks carry clearly over the steady rhythm of the steel rails.
- **Observation & Clue Mechanics:** The straight central aisle maintains clean sightlines from door to door. Heavy vestibule latches click with a distinct double snap whenever someone enters or departs. Curved window glass reflects passenger silhouettes against the exterior darkness, exposing movements behind seated travellers. Recessed brass rings on banquette tables hold drink glasses firmly in place, preserving beverage levels and coasters to reveal when a guest vacated their seat.
