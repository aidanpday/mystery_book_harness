# Step 01: Setting — Direction 03 (Non-Stop Mountain Express Train)

## Goal
Define a bounded "bottle" setting and detail a single representative zone aboard a high-speed mountain express train operating on a point-to-point run with zero intermediate stops.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** A rapid passenger express traversing steep mountain viaducts on a non-stop transit schedule. The mystery begins shortly after departure and must be solved before the train pulls into the terminus station.
- **Craft Emulation:** Agatha Christie (*Murder on the Orient Express*)—exploiting rigid timetable discipline, narrow corridor geometry, and strict linear movement where every passenger's transit between cars is a spatial puzzle.
- **Containment Mechanism:** High physical velocity through wilderness terrain without a single station stop between origin and destination; exterior car doors are keyed shut by the conductor during transit.

## Isolated Unit Scope
- **1 brief overview** of the express train (route duration, physical barriers to entry/exit, high-speed non-stop timetable).
- **1 detailed zone** within the passenger consist (e.g., The Observation Lounge Car: panoramic curved glass, banquettes, bar counter, double vestibule doors).
- **Length cap:** 250–350 words total.

## Universal Scientific Controls
- **Tone:** 40% on the stylistic calibration scale (crisp, witty, elegant whodunnit; articulate travellers; no morose melodrama).
- **Universal Bans:** Strictly zero mentions of ticket prices, inheritances, passenger wealth/debts, or financial gain; zero railway regulatory codes or legal statutes.
- **Fair-Play Clues:** Ground clues in linear car sightlines, vestibule latch clicks, carpeted footstep cadences, reflection angles on curved glass windows, and drink glass placements.
- **Style Blacklist:** Strictly ban all blacklisted words from `1_harness/03_style.md` (e.g., *humming, hum, palpable, tapestry, testament, unyielding, structural, shiver down the spine, cacophony, unspoken, visceral, labyrinthine, dance of shadows, pregnant pause, chill in the air, needle in a haystack, echoing silence, mechanics, mechanical, automatic, automatically, operational*).
