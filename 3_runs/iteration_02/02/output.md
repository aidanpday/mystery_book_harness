### Bounded Location Concept: Lyceum Playhouse (The Final Problem)
The Lyceum Playhouse features an ornate proscenium auditorium wrapped by a curved balcony, served by a central marble staircase and a mahogany lobby refreshment stand. The resident troupe is performing an adaptation of *The Final Problem*, featuring the twist that Moriarty is a fiction fabricated by Holmes to excuse his own blunders. As the Reichenbach falls scene begins, a fatal shot from the dark wings kills Holmes instantly. The audience applauds the apparent gunshot cue. Watson spots an engraved silver pocket watch dropped against the edge of the proscenium baseboards. If Watson breaks character and calls a halt, the backstage crew will rush into the wings and the killer can scoop up the dropped watch. Watson immediately assumes the role of sole detective, using improvised dialogue to keep every cast member fixed to their stage marks.

### Sample Zone Profile: Upstage Fly Floor & Pin Rail Catwalk
- **Role & Layout:** The fly floor is a narrow pine gallery bolted twenty feet above the upstage brick wall. A heavy timber pin rail runs its full thirty-foot length, bristling with wooden belaying pins that secure the hemp lines supporting painted backdrops and lighting bars. A vertical iron ladder at stage right provides the single access point, while a waist-high iron handrail borders the platform edge above the playing floor.
- **Occupant Dynamics:** Two flymen manage the lines during set changes, reading handwritten cue chalkboards hung from the rail. Cast members rarely climb the iron ladder unless fetching stored roping or checking overhead rigging. Movement along the platform requires quiet stepping along a coconut-matting runner to prevent loose dust or floorboard creaks from drifting down to the audience seated below during quiet dramatic scenes.
- **Observation & Clues:** The fly gallery affords a clear top-down view of both wings, the prompt corner, and the illuminated center stage. A chalk mark on pin seven shows where the alpine backdrop line was tied off during afternoon rehearsal; tonight, pin seven sits empty and the rope is knotted around an unnumbered support stanchion, directly above where Holmes fell, with fresh rosin dust sprinkled across the iron ladder rungs.
