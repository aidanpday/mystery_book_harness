# Step 01: Setting — Direction 02 (The Final Problem / Dropped Pocket Watch)

## Goal
Define the bounded "bottle" theater setting and detail a single representative elevated backstage zone for a repertory production where the actor playing Sherlock Holmes is killed during opening night, forcing the actor playing Dr. John Watson to keep the play moving to trap the killer who left physical evidence behind lit scenery.

## Direction Focus & Emulated Style
- **Artistic Direction:** The Lyceum Playhouse staging an adaptation of *The Final Problem*. In this production's scripted twist, Sherlock Holmes has completely invented Professor Moriarty to cover up an earlier unsolved failure. The actor playing Holmes is murdered from offstage to avenge a rival's destroyed theatrical career.
- **Craft Emulation:** *The Roman Hat Mystery* by Ellery Queen — rigorous spatial deductions, strict architectural geometry, and fair-play tracking of a missing or dropped physical object (an engraved pocket watch) across clearly defined theater zones.
- **Containment Mechanism:** Watson spots the killer's dropped engraved pocket watch beside the proscenium arch just behind the scenery line. Stopping the play would allow the cast and backstage crew to scatter, giving the killer an immediate opportunity to pick up the watch or blend into the dressing room corridors. Keeping the play running compels every actor to remain on their spike marks and in full view of the stage and wings.
