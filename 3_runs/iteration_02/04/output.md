### Bounded Location Concept: Criterion Playhouse (Silver Blaze)
The Criterion Playhouse encompasses a tiered horseshoe auditorium overlooking a wide thrust stage, backed by a carpeted entrance lobby with a central refreshment bar and twin flights of brass-railed stairs. The troupe is staging an adaptation of *Silver Blaze*, anchored by the twist that Holmes himself stole the prize racehorse. During Act Two, the actor playing Holmes—an arrogant understudy who took the role after deliberately tripping the veteran company lead on a staircase—is shot dead from the wings. The unsuspecting crowd laughs, assuming the collapse is part of the horse-theft farce. Realizing an abrupt curtain will prompt an immediate crush into the lobby and ruin the troupe, the actor playing Watson beckons the limping veteran lead out from the wings on a walking cane, ad-libbing dialogue to keep the show alive while deducing who pulled the trigger.

### Sample Zone Profile: Greenroom Prop Counter & Stage Door Vestibule
- **Role & Layout:** The greenroom is a whitewashed rectangular room situated between the stage-right wings and the backstage alley exit. A zinc-topped counter against the east wall holds props, makeup tins, and a water pitcher. Opposite the counter, four wicker armchairs surround a low coffee table, while an interior vestibule door with frosted glass panels separates the room from the exterior stage entrance and the doorkeeper's wooden stool.
- **Occupant Dynamics:** Cast members rest in the armchairs between scenes, sipping water and reading paperback scripts. The property master checks items off a clipboard ledger at the zinc counter, handing out riding crops, fake revolvers, and stage lanterns as cues approach. Actors entering from the drafty alleyway must wipe their shoes on a coir mat and leave their street coats on wall pegs beside the vestibule door.
- **Observation & Clues:** A clear sightline runs through the open greenroom doorway into the stage-right wing where actors await entrances. During afternoon rehearsal, the property table held two starter pistols with green tape on their handles; tonight, one pistol sits in the zinc basin smelling of burnt cordite, while an ungreased tallow candle taken from the greenroom table matches the residue found earlier on the dressing room stairs.
