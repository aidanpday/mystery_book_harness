### Bounded Location Concept: Sovereign Repertory Theatre (A Scandal in Bohemia)
The Sovereign Repertory Theatre centers on a two-tier auditorium facing a raked proscenium stage, fronted by a wide lobby with a central beverage counter and double staircases rising to the dress circle. The company is staging an adaptation of *A Scandal in Bohemia*, wherein Irene Adler conspires directly with Dr. John Watson against Sherlock Holmes. During Act Two, an offstage gunshot drops the actor playing Holmes dead in the prompt corner. The seated audience assumes the report belongs to the drama, while the cast freezes in terror. Realizing an early curtain would panic five hundred patrons into the lobby and allow the killer to slip out the front portico unnoticed, the actor playing Watson delivers an impromptu line, stepping forward to steer Irene through an altered investigation that holds every suspect pinned to their marks until the final bow.

### Sample Zone Profile: Prompt Wing & Quick-Change Screen
- **Role & Layout:** The prompt wing sits behind the stage-left proscenium flat, six feet from the main playing space. A slanted pine desk holds the prompt script and a switch panel with red and green cue lights. Beside the desk, a three-panel canvas changing screen shields a vanity shelf and mirror, where actors make thirty-second costume adjustments without returning to the basement dressing rooms. Two stage cables run along the floorboards toward the dimmer racks.
- **Occupant Dynamics:** The deputy stage manager remains seated at the desk throughout each act, signaling entrances with the cue switches. Cast members step into the wing two minutes before their entrances, checking props on the side table or adjusting neckties behind the canvas screen. During fast transitions, actors brush past stagehands resetting furniture flats, maintaining absolute silence while the onstage dialogue continues five yards away.
- **Observation & Clues:** The prompt desk commands a clear diagonal sightline across the playing area to the stage-right wing. Green cue lamps illuminate anyone waiting at the prop table. During afternoon rehearsal, Irene's cue came from a brass carriage horn hung beside the script desk; tonight the horn sits displaced behind the costume screen, while fresh powder residue coats the bottom pine shelf beneath the sound cue switches.
