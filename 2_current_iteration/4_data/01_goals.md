# Project goals

The steps should establish the fabula (in-universe truth for what happened), then the list of scenes focuses on the syuzhet (reader experience of solving the crime). Both the fabula and the syuzhet must be compelling individually.

Early steps (location, characters, clues) should focus more on fabula. The crime must be possible and understandable.

Later steps (scenes, story beats, chapter drafting) should focus exclusively on syuzhet. Ensure that the reader has every physical clue, timeline anomaly, and behavioral tell observed by the protagonist. It is vital that the fabula is fair, despite being obscured in subtle ways. Details of the crime are not be fully shown to the reader until the end of the novel.

The story advances through investigation and deduction.

## Outline

### Fabula

C-01 is a fraudster who is smuggling a counterfeit MacGuffin. C-01 is pursued by C-03 who intends to steal the MacGuffin to expose C-01 to an authoritative panel at the destination. Police are available to contact but cannot interfere with they arrive at the destination.

There are two known disruptions during the journey, where the entire location briefly goes dark. During the first disruption, C-03 steals the MacGuffin. C-01 plays a victim and investigator, charming C-02 into helping discover the thief. Because the MacGuffin is missing, nobody can question the legitimacy of the macguffin.

C-01 and C-02 investigate until the second disruption, when most characters scramble to hide their personal secrets. C-01 finishes their investigation and recovers the MacGuffin. C-03 exposes that the MacGuffin is fake, and everyone (including C-02) begin to question C-01.

C-01 assures everyone that the police are waiting for them anyway, and they are near the destination. However, as they arrive, C-01 slips away early with the MacGuffin. C-03 is punished by police. C-02 is lauded for their work discovering the truth.

### Syuzhet

The story is told from C-02's perspective. They are not initially connected to either C-01 or C-03. C-02 is the social inferior of C-01 and C-03, and they are pleasantly surprised that C-01 treats them with respect and curiosity. C-02 helps the investigation earnestly.

C-02 has mixed feelings about C-01 upon learning about the fraud. C-02 feels dissatisfied because their investigation worked but it resulted in the true culprit escaping, and justice being mis-served.

## Inspiration

  - NAME: The Usual Suspects (1995)
    POSITIVE_EXAMPLE: Builds internal mystique with targetted jargon that unravels in a satisfying way. Main characters dance among the group and the framing device. Defies expectations in clear ways, establishing clear sources of truth (the result at the boat, the faxed sketch).
    NEGATIVE_EXAMPLE: Unreliable narrator. Flashbacks.
  - NAME: Clue (1985)
    POSITIVE_EXAMPLE: Colorful cast of characters that mix fun and mystery in clear ways. Witty dialogue. Enclosed location.
    NEGATIVE_EXAMPLE: Multiple possible endings. Self referential. Overly silly.
  - NAME: Twin Peaks (1990)
    POSITIVE_EXAMPLE: Atmospheric with a lot of mystique. Realistic town imagery with interwoven subplots between robust characters.
    NEGATIVE_EXAMPLE: No clear resolution to the mystery.
  - NAME: Arsène Lupin, Gentleman Burglar (1907)
    POSITIVE_EXAMPLE: Clever, charming, and procedural with its puzzle-like crimes.
    NEGATIVE_EXAMPLE: Describes planning a heist when we are solving a mystery.
  - NAME: Murder on the Orient Express (1934)
    POSITIVE_EXAMPLE: Book that takes place in an enclosed societal microcosm. Classic whodunit investigation.
    NEGATIVE_EXAMPLE: The entire cast is not guilty in this story. The train has stops that allow characters to enter the bounds of the story.
  - NAME: The Hound of the Baskervilles (1902)
    POSITIVE_EXAMPLE: Investigation tactics. An interesting mystery. The story is told via a straightman sidekick to the main detective.
    NEGATIVE_EXAMPLE: Detective is a brilliant savant in all fields.
  - NAME: Train to Busan
    POSITIVE_EXAMPLE: Movie with one main location (the train) and clear start- and end-points. It begins with people getting on a train on a regular day, and ends when the train arrives. Social rules are established with a sense of normalcy, and the dynamic changes dramatically as zombies appear. Characters are changed by the arrival.
    NEGATIVE_EXAMPLE: Zombies, horror, and action scenes do not suit this story.
  - NAME: The Lighthouse (2019)
    POSITIVE_EXAMPLE: Stylish movie about the interplay between two people. Uses one central location. Dialogue is personalized to the setting and to the characters.
    NEGATIVE_EXAMPLE: The setting and tone do not suit this story.
