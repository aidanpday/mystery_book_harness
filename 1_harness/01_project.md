# Project

Whodunnit mystery concept. Rather than drafting a complete novella end-to-end, this project prototypes each step of the mystery-writing process in isolation on its smallest testable unit.

## Target audience

Focus on intrigue and fun. The mystery should be possible but difficult to solve for an attentive reader. Physical and social clues must be visible to the reader, yet subtle. Use misdirection so their implications are not obvious.

Focus on the reader experience. Use three target audience profiles:
  - Smart teenager who wants to challenge themselves
  - Busy professional who wants to relax
  - Parent who needs a breezy read but wants to ponder it

## Scope

The underlying story is a "bottle" mystery with one main bounded location, which has clear parameters for entering and leaving. The characters are all people who would normally be there. When the crime is discovered, it reframes their normal interactions before. The mystery is solved just before the characters leave the main location. Act 1 can include characters before entering the location.

Every prototype tests only a single isolated unit of one step (e.g., one setting zone, one character, one clue chain, or one short scene). Details generated in any step must serve the reader's enjoyment or the artistic value of the story.

## AI experimentation rules

All prototypes are generated by AI agents under controlled experimental conditions:
  - **Same model per experiment:** Use the exact same cost-effective model across all 10 prototypes in an iteration (and across iterations of the same step) to guarantee apples-to-apples comparisons.
  - **Concise, single-unit outputs:** Keep deliverables brief and strictly scoped to the step's smallest unit so a human can quickly read and compare 10 outputs side-by-side.
  - **Single-step execution:** Each prototype executes at most one set of minimal instructions in a fresh, isolated context, producing a single final deliverable with no intermediate outputs, rework loops, or change logs.
  - **Output as proxy:** Overall polish is not the objective; outputs exist to evaluate the promise of the chosen artistic direction and the leverage of its generated instructions.
