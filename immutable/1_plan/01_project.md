# Project

Whodunnit mystery novella. The final output is between 10,000 and 20,000 words.

## Target audience

Focus on intrigue and fun. The mystery should be possible but difficult to solve for an attentive reader. Physical and social clues must be visible to the reader, yet subtle, Use misdirection so their implications are not obvious.

Focus on the reader experience. Use three target audience profiles:
  - Smart teenager who wants to challenge themselves
  - Busy professional who wants to relax
  - Parent who needs a breezy read but wants to ponder it

## Scope

This is a "bottle" story with one main location, which has clear parameters for entering and leaving. The characters are all people who would normally be there. When the crime is discovered, it reframes their normal interactions before. The mystery is solved just before the characters leave the main location. Act 1 can include characters before entering the location.

Data from intermediate steps must serve the reader's enjoyment or the artistic value of the story, or else be removed or refactored.

## AI use

This entire project is generated by AI agents.
  - Use cost-effective models.
  - Generate a plan for every step before starting work.
    - Plans are not stored permanently. They are always approved.
    - Plans must specify all decisions and intended steps.
  - After every step, two skills run:
    - First, it is strictly required to run `2_steps/quality_check.md`.
    - Lastly, it is strictly required to generate an entry in the change log, adhering to `1_plan/03_change_log.md`.
